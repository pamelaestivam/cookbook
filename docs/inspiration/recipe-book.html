Cakes & celebration

Naked Cake with Ninho Milk Brigadeiro

A Brazilian three-layer naked cake with a tender vanilla crumb, generous powdered-milk brigadeiro filling, piped rosettes, and fresh strawberries, blueberries, and raspberries.

3 × 18 cm / 7 in pans 180°C / 350°F 35 min bake
Watch the original Cakepedia video ↗

Mise en place

Ingredients

Cake batter

  • 200 g unsalted butter, at room temperature
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla essence
  • 4 cups wheat flour
  • 200 ml milk
  • 20 g baking powder

Brigadeiro filling

  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk (395 g)
  • 4 tablespoons Ninho powdered milk
  • 1 box heavy cream / creme de leite (200 g)

From the video: the presenter doubled this filling recipe for the cake. The quantities above make one base recipe.

Decoration

  • Fresh strawberries
  • Fresh blueberries
  • Fresh raspberries

Method

Make the cake

Cake batter

  1. Beat the butter briefly to soften it, add the 2 cups of sugar, and beat until light and fluffy.

  2. Add the 4 eggs and beat very well until you get a fluffy, whitish cream.

  3. Add 1 tablespoon of vanilla essence and keep beating.

  4. Add half of the flour (of the 4 cups total), mixing with a whisk.

  5. Add half of the milk (of the 200 ml total) and mix until homogeneous.

  6. Sift in the remaining flour and mix; add the remaining milk and mix until very homogeneous.

  7. Add the 20 g of baking powder last and stir until fully incorporated. The batter will be fairly firm.

  8. Divide the batter among three 18 cm (about 7 in) pans greased with butter and floured; smooth the tops with a spatula.

  9. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C (350°F) for 35 minutes. Let cool and level off the domes.

Brigadeiro filling

  1. In a pan over low heat, add the condensed milk and the 4 tablespoons of Ninho powdered milk; stir.

  2. Add the heavy cream and stir constantly so it doesn’t burn or stick to the pan.

  3. Cook until it thickens and starts to come away from the bottom of the pan, then turn off the heat.

  4. Transfer to a glass bowl and let it cool completely before assembling.

Assembly

  1. On a cake board, place a little brigadeiro, then set down one cake layer.

  2. Put the brigadeiro in a piping bag; pipe dollops around the edge of the cake—squeeze and pull back—all the way around.

  3. Fill the center generously with filling and spread with a spatula.

  4. Optional: moisten the cake very lightly, but don’t overdo it—the cake needs to keep its structure.

  5. Place the next layer and repeat the edge dollops and center filling, then add the final layer and straighten it.

  6. With a 1M piping tip, pipe roses on top in a full circle—squeeze and turn. Between each rose, pipe a small pitanguinha dollop: squeeze, stop, and pull.

  7. Decorate the top with the strawberries, blueberries, and raspberries.

Chocolate & brownies

Flourless Brownie (Brownie Sem Farinha)

A flourless brownie recipe test from Almanaque SOS, baked by the host alongside a guest with 20 years of experience as a pastry chef.

5 ingredients No flour Eggs weighed to 165 g
Watch the original Almanaque SOS Short ↗

Mise en place

Ingredients

Brownie batter

  • 210 g bittersweet chocolate (chocolate meio amargo)
  • 75 g refined sugar
  • 60 g brown sugar
  • 165 g eggs, weighed
  • 24 g 100% cocoa powder

Use real chocolate: not chocolate-flavored coating (cobertura). There is no flour and no separate butter—the cocoa mixes into the natural fat of the melted chocolate.

Method

Make the brownie

Mix & bake

  1. Preheat the oven.

  2. Weigh all ingredients precisely—especially the eggs, which should weigh 165 g rather than be counted.

  3. Melt the 210 g of bittersweet chocolate and whisk until smooth.

  4. Mix the eggs with the refined and brown sugars, then combine with the melted chocolate.

  5. Add the 24 g of 100% cocoa powder. It will mix much better into the fat of the chocolate.

  6. Pour the batter into the pan and bake. In the video, it baked for 15 minutes at 170°C (338°F) in a professional oven. For a home oven, use 180°C (350°F) if yours runs powerful or 220°C (428°F) if it is weaker—you’ll need to test it. Keep an eye on the crust, as every oven is different.

  7. Expect some sugar specks that did not fully dissolve in the finished brownie; the video notes that this is not a mistake.

Breakfast & protein

Protein Pancakes

Cottage cheese and eggs make these pancakes genuinely filling—about 10 g of protein each, with mini blueberries folded through the batter. The full batch makes 30; divide by three for a normal batch.

Per pancake · ~122 cal ~10 g protein ~12.5 g carbs ~3.5 g fat

Ingredients

Makes 30 pancakes

Full batch

  • 6 cups low-fat cottage cheese
  • 12 whole eggs
  • 3 tsp vanilla extract
  • 12 Tbsp sugar-free maple syrup
  • 3.75 cups self-rising flour
  • 300 g mini blueberries

Normal batch · divide by 3 (makes 10)

  • 2 cups low-fat cottage cheese
  • 4 whole eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 Tbsp sugar-free maple syrup
  • 1.25 cups self-rising flour
  • 100 g mini blueberries

Notes

From the recipe card

Nutrition, per pancake

~122 calories ~10 g protein ~12.5 g carbs ~3.5 g fat

Macros are as listed on the recipe card; its batch note reads “divide by 3 for a normal batch,” worked out in the ingredient list.
